I was born in Seoul but grew up abroad, moving back to Seoul after high school. For most of my life, English was my primary language, but now (hopefully) I am fully bilingual. Outside of research, I love traveling (especially for great views like skylines and the ocean), discovering good food spots, hitting the gym, listening to music (I somehow have 218 playlists...!), and spending time around animals. I also enjoy meeting new people, so feel free to reach out to talk about research or life!
