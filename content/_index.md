I am interested in pushing the boundaries of humanity. Currently, I am an intern at Amazon AGI Foundations, previously at LG AI Research (Superintelligence Lab) and Trillion Labs. I have published papers in collaboration with researchers at Google DeepMind, Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology, and LG AI Research (EXAONE Lab). My work spans efficient long-context inference, scaling pre-training, post-training, feedback and evaluation, continual learning, co-evolution, and (M)OPD.
